How does a youth access SIFA?

  1. The youth dials *301#.
  2. A magic link arrives on their phone by SMS from Wasiliana.
  3. The youth taps the link. It opens a browser and takes them straight into SIFA.
Good to knowThe magic link logs the youth in. They do not need a username or password. If the link is not tapped promptly or gets lost, see missing or expired magic link.

Add SIFA to the Home Screen

Do this once SIFA is open in the browser. The youth can then find SIFA again from an app icon.

Standard method (most phones)

  1. Tap the three dots in the top right corner of the browser.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. On the "Get SIFA on your phone" page, scroll and tap Install SIFA App.
  4. Wait a few seconds. The icon appears on the Home Screen.

Alternative method (mostly Samsung phones)

Use this if "Add to Home Screen" is missing or does not work.

  1. With SIFA open in the browser, tap the three vertical lines at the bottom right of the screen.
  2. Tap Add current webpage to.
  3. Select Home Screen.
  4. When asked "Add to Home page?", tap Add. Installation begins.
Field tipAndroid skins label this differently. If neither menu matches, look for wording like "Install app", "Add shortcut" or "Add page to". They do the same thing.

If the icon will not add, the layout may be locked. See Home Screen locked.

The Home Screen layout is locked and the icon will not add

Either route works.

Route 1: through the Home Screen

  1. Long-press empty space on the Home Screen until the popup menu appears.
  2. Tap Home screen settings.
  3. Toggle off Lock Home screen layout. These two options are usually next to each other.

Route 2: faster, if the phone has settings search

  1. Open Settings and tap the search bar at the top.
  2. Type "lock home screen".
  3. Tap the result and toggle it off.

Then go back to Add SIFA to the Home Screen.

SIFA is stuck on a loading screen

This is almost always one of two causes: an outdated browser, or depleted Okoa Data. If SIFA worked before and suddenly stopped, check Okoa Data first.

Cause 1: outdated browser

Check the Chrome version.

  1. Open the Chrome app.
  2. Tap the three dots (top right), then Settings.
  3. Scroll to About Chrome.

If the version is below 100:

  1. Update the browser from the Play Store.
  2. Recommend Google Chrome as the default browser for SIFA.
  3. Reopen the magic link, or send a new one, once it is updated.
Why this worksSIFA relies on modern browser features. Older or lesser-known pre-installed browsers often cannot display it, which causes an endless loading screen. Encourage every youth to use Chrome from the start.

Cause 2: Okoa Data has run out

SIFA runs on sponsored data. When Okoa Data is depleted the phone shows as disconnected. The magic link is often still valid. The connection just cannot reach the sponsored data.

  1. Ask: "Were you able to access SIFA before, and are you currently on Okoa Data or airtime?"
  2. Access returns once the Okoa balance is paid off.
Good to knowThis is a data-access issue. It is not a SIFA problem and not a magic link problem.

The youth gets a 404 error when opening the link

A 404 means the magic link is broken. A common cause is a full stop at the end of the link. Fix it in either of two ways.

  1. Dial *301# again to receive a new magic link.
  2. Use the Registration app. It has an option to generate the magic link directly, and you can share that with the youth.

The youth never got a magic link, or it has expired

Generate a new link in the CEA app. Do not ask the youth to dial *301# again.

  1. Open the CEA app.
  2. Tap Verify Youth.
  3. Scroll down to Youth Login Link.
  4. Tap it. A new magic link is generated.
  5. Share the link with the youth so they can log back in.
Check before sharingConfirm you are sending the correct link to the correct youth. A misdirected magic link can log the wrong person into someone else's account.

"Continue with Google" does nothing on the Registration app

This happens on the SIFA Registration app. Clearing all browsing data, set to "All time", usually fixes it. There are two methods.

Method 1: through the Chrome app

  1. Open Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots (top right), then History.
  3. Tap Delete browsing data.
  4. Set the time range to All time.
  5. Make sure Browsing history, Cookies and site data and Cached images and files are ticked.
  6. Tap Clear data to confirm.

Method 2: through the phone's Settings app

  1. Open Settings, not the browser.
  2. Tap Apps, then find and tap Chrome.
  3. Tap Storage & cache.
  4. Tap Clear data (or "Clear storage").
  5. Confirm the warning. This signs the youth out of all sites.
Which to useMethod 1 lets you pick what to clear, so it is the safer default. Method 2 is faster but wipes saved logins and preferences for the whole app. Use it only when the youth cannot find or use the in-browser menu.
